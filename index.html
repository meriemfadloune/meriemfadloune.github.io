02 / CREATIVE PROCESS

FROM RESEARCH
TO OBJECT.

01

Research & observe

Explore Moroccan artisanal heritage, geometry and architecture. Define the context, the user and the intention.

02

Sketch & experiment

Translate references into form. Study proportions, colour, texture and the relationship between material and function.

03

Develop & construct

Work through patterns, assembly and material choices. Use prototyping to question and refine the design.

04

Refine & resolve

Consider the object in use. Adjust the details, the finish and the construction to bring the idea into focus.

MAROQUINERIE DESIGNER

Contemporary design.
Rooted in craft.

I am a third-year Design en Maroquinerie student at the Académie des Arts Traditionnels in Casablanca, Morocco.

My creative practice connects Moroccan artisanal heritage with contemporary leather goods design. Geometric and architectural references shape my research, while material experimentation and traditional leatherworking guide the development of each object.

I am interested in the point where creative vision, technical expertise and craftsmanship meet: imagining a product, understanding its construction and bringing it into physical form.
